# Gardens by the Bay

## Gardens and conservatories

Gardens by the Bay includes the Flower Dome in its Bay South area. The Flower Dome covers 1.28 hectares and displays plants originating on five continents. Cloud Forest contains an indoor waterfall.

## Supertrees

The Supertrees support vertical planting. Their functions include harvesting rainwater, producing solar energy and ventilating conservatories. Visitors can use the OCBC Skyway between two of these structures. Garden Rhapsody is a light-and-sound attraction associated with the Supertrees.
