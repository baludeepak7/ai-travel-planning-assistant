# National Gallery Singapore

## Location and architecture

National Gallery Singapore occupies the former Supreme Court and City Hall in the Civic District. Its architecture includes atriums and roofing made with glass and metal, allowing daylight into the building.

## Art collection

The gallery focuses on Southeast Asian art. VisitSingapore describes a collection exceeding 8,000 works, spanning the nineteenth century onward. The venue occupies 64,000 square metres.
